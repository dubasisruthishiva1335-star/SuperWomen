## 1. Executive Summary & The Problem

In India, over **280 million women** travel daily for college, work, healthcare, and leisure. However:
- **84% of women** report feeling unsafe when traveling alone late in the evening or early morning.
- Traditional ride-hailing platforms (Uber, Ola, Rapido) have **< 1.5% female driver representation**, leading to persistent safety anxieties and driver misconduct incidents.
- Existing women drivers face male passenger harassment, forcing many to leave the gig economy prematurely.

**SuperWomen solves both sides of the marketplace with a zero-compromise, 100% women-only policy.**

## 4. Business Model & Unit Economics

SuperWomen operates on a transparent, driver-friendly take-rate model:

| Parameter | Traditional Platforms | SuperWomen Platform |
|---|---|---|
| **Commission / Take-Rate** | 25% – 30% | **12% – 15%** (Higher take-home for Captains) |
| **Driver Churn Rate** | 45% annually | **< 12%** (Higher retention due to safe passenger pool) |
| **Average Ride Fare (Bike)** | ₹80 – ₹120 | **₹85 – ₹145** |
| **Average Ride Fare (Auto)** | ₹140 – ₹220 | **₹145 – ₹240** |
| **Safety Fee (Pass)** | ₹0 | **₹5 / ride** (Funds 24/7 emergency response hub) |

### Projected Captain Earnings:
- **Part-time (4 hours / 8 rides daily):** **₹1,200 – ₹1,500 / day** (₹36,000 – ₹45,000 / month)
- **Full-time (8 hours / 16 rides daily):** **₹2,400 – ₹3,000 / day** (₹72,000 – ₹90,000 / month)

## 5. Go-To-Market (GTM) Plan: 60-Day Warangal & Hyderabad Launch

### Phase 1: Captain Acquisition & Community (Days 1–20)
- Partner with local women self-help groups (SHGs), colleges, and women driving schools.
- Onboard first **50 verified Captains** with subsidized helmets, phone mounts, and free safety training.

### Phase 2: College & Tech Park Activation (Days 21–40)
- Launch campus ambassador campaigns across universities and tech corridors (e.g. Hitec City, Gachibowli, Kakatiya University).
- Referral campaign: *"Invite a Sister / Friend, get ₹100 ride credit."*

### Phase 3: Hyper-Local Density & City Expansion (Days 41–60)
- Achieve < 4 minute pickup ETA in target clusters.
- Expand from Warangal to Hyderabad and Bangalore.
